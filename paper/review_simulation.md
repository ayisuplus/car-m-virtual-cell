# Review simulation — CAR-M Simulator (Nature-family protocol)

> Simulated peer review. Three reviewer reports (emphasis only differs) + one cross-review synthesis + a consolidated unsupported-claims register. Every substantive concern carries a stable ID, a faithful `claim_pointer`, and a verifiable `evidence_pointer`. No identities, institutions, or CVs are invented. No p-values, sample sizes, degrees of freedom, confidence intervals, or software versions are fabricated — where absent they are marked `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED` or "absent". This is judged as the computational sandbox / hypothesis-generation tool it *claims* to be, not as a wet-lab validation study.

# Review setup

## Shared manuscript fact base

- **Claimed object.** "CAR-M Simulator", a browser-based, client-side agent-based model (ABM) of the tumour microenvironment (TME): four agent types (CAR-macrophage, WT macrophage, tumour, CD8⁺ T-cell), a 10-factor explicit-finite-difference reaction–diffusion field, a checkpoint-aware phagocytosis module, and a 6→32→32→3 MLP "neural surrogate" that replaces per-cell ODE polarization integration (§3, §4).
- **Claimed quantitative result.** Surrogate **1.5 µs** vs RK4 ODE **12 µs** per cell-decision, "**~8×**" speedup; per-frame (N=55) **0.08 ms vs 0.66 ms**, "~206×" / "~25×" 60-fps headroom (§5.1, Table 2, Fig 2). Derived from a *deterministic micro-benchmark*, 200,000 evaluations/method, *not* biological replicates.
- **Claimed qualitative results.** Five fixed-seed (20250706) scripted scenarios showing *directional* trends: checkpoint blockade gates phagocytosis; CD147 switches mechanism to ECM degradation; HER2-low weakens phagocytosis; high TGF-β modest; M1 classification & CD8⁺ activation stay near baseline (so not plotted as time series) (§5.2, Figs 3–4).
- **Claimed significance.** A real-time, reproducible, open, in-browser workbench for *mechanistic trend exploration, hypothesis generation, and teaching* — explicitly **not** clinical prediction (§1.3, §6.3).
- **Likely readership.** Computational immunology / systems-biology methods readers; immunotherapy engineers wanting an interactive design sandbox; educators. For a *Nature-family* methods venue the natural home would be *Nature Computational Science* / *Nature Methods* (tool/Resource); realistically the work is currently at *JOSS / SoftwareX / F1000Research / bioRxiv methods-note* standard.
- **Visible limitations (self-disclosed, §7).** 2D domain; modest cell counts; surrogate ground-truth is a *synthetic ODE*, not measured data; several parameters plausibility-based; performance is single-machine JS.
- **Reproducibility posture (self-disclosed + verified by reviewer on disk).** Fixed seed → bit-for-bit runs; `scripts/benchmark-surrogate.mjs` ships; figure pipeline `plot_all_figures.py` + `export_source_data.py` ship; Source Data CSVs for Figs 2–4 ship; vector (SVG/PDF) **and** TIFF masters ship for all four figures.

# Reviewer 1 — emphasis: technical soundness & reproducibility

**Overall assessment.** The engineering is real and the reproducibility scaffolding is unusually good for a draft: deterministic engine, shipped benchmark, shipped Source Data, shipped vector/TIFF masters and figure scripts. As a *software/methods artifact* it is close. As a *quantitative scientific claim-set* it has several internal-consistency and numerical-methods defects that must be fixed before the numbers can be trusted at face value. The most serious are (i) an undefined surrogate-fidelity metric, (ii) a benchmark reported without any timing variance while mis-using the "not a biological replicate" argument to justify that omission, (iii) a latent numerical-instability in the diffusion solver at high user speed, and (iv) scenario definitions that cannot be reproduced from the paper.

**Who would care and why.** Computational-modelling groups building interactive immunology sandboxes; anyone who wants to drop a learned surrogate into a per-agent hot path and needs an honest benchmarking template; educators. They will value the determinism and the shipped scripts, but will be burned by the missing scenario configs and the undefined accuracy number if they try to reuse the surrogate idea rigorously.

**Major strengths.** (S1) Determinism + shipped benchmark + shipped Source Data + vector/TIFF masters is best-practice and rare at draft stage. (S2) The surrogate is honestly scoped: ground truth = the ODE, *not* data (§4.4, §6.2), and the older "847 ms vs 0.3 ms" figure is explicitly retracted (§5.1) — that self-correction is exactly the behaviour a methods venue rewards. (S3) The CD147 mechanism-change demonstration (Fig 3a–c: tumour grows, ECM drops, phagocytosis = 0) is a clean, internally consistent proof that the construct levers change *mechanism*, not just rate.

**Major concerns.** R1-M1..R1-M4 below.

**Technical flaws to resolve first.** R1-M1 (stability), R1-M2 (accuracy metric), R1-M4 (scenario configs) — these can change or void presented numbers if left as-is.

**Assessment against the venue.** Below a *Nature-family* methods bar as written, mainly because the headline quantitative claims (8×, 96%, the directional §5.2 bullets) rest on definitions/measurements that are not yet reproducible or are internally inconsistent. At *JOSS / SoftwareX / bioRxiv* it is a credible, fixable submission.

**Recommended posture.** **Major revision** with a short, mechanical fix-list; no conceptual redesign required.

- **R1-M1** | technical soundness | claim_pointer: §4.1 "`dt = min(Δt_ms / 1000, 0.05) × speed`"; §4.3 "diffusion coefficient `D = 1000` for soluble factors" on "grid with spacing 40 units", explicit five-point Laplacian. | evidence_pointer: §4.3 (no CFL guard stated anywhere); §4.1 (speed is an unbounded user multiplier). | concern: 2D explicit FTCS is stable only for r = D·dt/h² ≤ 0.25. None of the presented figures appear affected (they look like default-speed runs), so this does *not* invalidate the paper's results, but it is a latent correctness bug in an "interactive" tool. | resolution test: either (a) sub-step the field so each sub-step satisfies r ≤ 0.25 regardless of `speed`, or (b) clamp/limit `speed` and document the bound, and (c) add a unit test that runs every scenario at max speed and asserts all field values stay in [0,1] and finite.

# Reviewer 2 — emphasis: framing honesty, over-claim risk & figure quality

**Overall assessment.** The authors' *meta*-honesty is the paper's single best feature and it is largely sustained: §1.3, §6.3, the Limitations, and the figure legends all hold the "trend tool, not predictor" line, and the retraction of the old 847 ms number is exemplary. However, honesty is a property of *every sentence*, and it is not 100% uniform: (a) there is a concrete numerical labelling error (the "106" tumour count) that propagates from a figure legend into the results narrative; (b) one of the four §5.2 "directional" bullets (checkpoint blockade) is not actually corroborated — and is arguably contradicted — by the only figure that varies blockade; and (c) the abstract over-states what was timed ("whole scene interactive at 60 fps"). None of these are fraudulent; all are fixable. The figures are clean and the spatial panels are well encoded, but the line panels are colour-only and two of the six advertised dashboard readouts never appear.

**Who would care and why.** Readers who will cite this as evidence that "computation shows CD47 blockade helps CAR-M" or "CD147 leaves 106 tumour cells" — i.e. anyone downstream of the abstract/figure legends. They need the legends to be exactly right, because the body text is careful but the legends and abstract are what get quoted.

**Major strengths.** (S1) Consistent, repeated, correctly-placed scope disclaimers; the explicit non-goal section (§1.3) and the "design anchor, not validation" note on CT-0508 (§6.3) are exactly right. (S2) Fig 4 uses shape **+** colour per cell type (good practice; rare and appreciated). (S3) The decision to *not* plot flat M1/CD8 panels, with an explicit legend explanation, is honest and correct — far better than padding the figure with null panels.

**Major concerns.** R2-M1..R2-M3.

**Technical flaws to resolve first.** R2-M1 (the 106/76 labelling) is a factual error in a legend and the results text; fix before anything else.

**Assessment against the venue.** The honest framing is *above* average for the venue; the legend/abstract inconsistencies are *below* it. Net: the framing is an asset that is currently being undercut by its own legends — fix the legends and the framing becomes a clean strength.

**Recommended posture.** **Major revision**, predominantly editorial/figure corrections plus one re-check of a mechanistic claim.

# Reviewer 3 — emphasis: scope, related work, calibration path & accessibility

**Overall assessment.** The scope is well-chosen and the boundary is honestly drawn; the contribution (real-time, in-browser, surrogate-accelerated, reproducible TME sandbox) is coherent and the CD147 demonstration shows the sandbox *does* let mechanism changes run on screen. The weaknesses are about *positioning and evidential weight*: the related work is too thin to establish novelty/positioning for a methods venue; the directional conclusions rest on a single seed and a single (partly assumed) parameterization with no sensitivity or uncertainty analysis — which is exactly the quantity a tool whose stated value is "which lever matters, and roughly how sensitive" ought to provide; and the calibration path, while correctly named as future work, is too vague to be a roadmap. Accessibility-wise the workbench itself is never shown.

**Who would care and why.** Labs deciding whether to *adopt or extend* this as a starting point, and reviewers assessing whether the "mechanism-inspired sandbox" niche is genuinely differentiated from existing ABM/ODE/surrogate tooling. They need positioning, sensitivity, and a believable calibration plan.

# Cross-review synthesis

**Consensus strengths.**
1. **Honesty is structural, not cosmetic.** §1.3, §6.3, the Limitations, the retracted 847-ms number, the "ODE is the ground truth not data" statement, and the refusal to plot flat null panels together form a consistency that most simulator drafts lack. This is the paper's defining asset and all three reviewers reward it.
2. **Reproducibility scaffolding is real and verified on disk** (deterministic seed, shipped benchmark, Source Data CSVs, vector+TIFF masters, figure/export scripts) — best-practice for a draft.
3. **The CD147 mechanism-switch demonstration** (Fig 3a–c) is a clean, internally consistent proof-of-value: the sandbox visibly turns a *rate* lever into a *mechanism* lever (tumour grows, ECM falls, phagocytosis = 0). This is the figure that justifies the tool's existence.
4. **The surrogate is deployed at the right bottleneck** and the engineering (inline Float32 weights, fused scalar loops, no GPU) is appropriate to the tiny network.

**Consensus technical risks.**
- **Internal-consistency error** in legends/narrative: the "106" tumour count is actually the *total* (tumour = 76) — R2-M1.
- **Headline surrogate fidelity** ("95.96% accuracy") is an undefined metric with no trajectory-level check — R1-M2.
- **The §5.2 checkpoint-blockade bullet is not corroborated** by Fig 3 (direction not visible / within seed noise) — R2-M2.
- **Single-seed point estimates** confound effect size with stochastic-engine noise; multi-seed envelopes are needed (not as biological replicates) — R3-M2 (also resolves R2-M2).
- **No sensitivity/uncertainty** over the assumed constants that drive the directional story — R3-M1.
- **Benchmark has no timing variance** (the "not a biological replicate" point was mis-extended to suppress *measurement* variance) — R1-M3.
- **Latent FTCS instability** at high user speed (uncapped `speed` vs CFL limit) — R1-M1.
- **Scenario configs not in the paper** ("CT-0508-inspired baseline" has no concrete vector) — R1-M4.

**Emphasis differences.** R1 centres on *numerical/ reproducibility correctness* (stability, metric definitions, configs, timing statistics). R2 centres on *whether the legends and abstract match the data and the careful body framing* (the 106/76 error, the CD47 direction, the 60-fps abstract line, figure accessibility). R3 centres on *evidential weight and positioning* (sensitivity, multi-seed robustness, related work, calibration concreteness, showing the UI). The three are complementary, not contradictory: R2's CD47 problem and R3's single-seed problem are the *same* underlying gap viewed from framing vs statistics, and the agreed fix (multi-seed envelopes + softened wording) closes both at once.

**Broad-significance read.** As written the manuscript is **below the Nature-family bar**, but the distance is *narrow and mechanical*: it is not a conceptual or honesty deficit (those are strong), it is (a) a handful of legend/abstract corrections, (b) undefined/un-quantified numbers that need proper metrics and variance, (c) the absence of any sensitivity/multi-seed/empirical anchor, and (d) thin positioning. After those, it is a solid **JOSS / SoftwareX / F1000Research / bioRxiv methods** paper; reaching a *Nature-family* tool venue would additionally require at least one empirical sanity anchor (even a qualitative macrophage-state-distribution check against a public atlas) and a UI figure — i.e. showing the tool connects to *something* measured, which the authors themselves name as the endgame. The authors should not be pushed toward over-claiming to close that gap; the honest "trend tool" identity is correct and should be preserved.

**The single most important open question before a strong case can be made.**
> *Do the directional separations between scenarios survive a multi-seed Monte-Carlo of the stochastic engine (model stochasticity, not biological replicates), **and** can at least one headline readout be anchored — even qualitatively — to a public dataset?*
The first half converts every §5.2 trend from "one seed" to "robust" and simultaneously repairs the unsupported CD47-direction claim; the second half is the minimal bridge from "mechanism-inspired sandbox" to "sandbox whose trends are consistent with measured biology." Neither requires abandoning the honest framing; both are cheap given the surrogate's speed. Without them, the tool is exactly what the authors say it is — a hypothesis-*generator* — but the generated hypotheses carry no stated robustness and no external tether.

The honest boundary is correct; the work required is to make every figure, legend, and number live up to it, and to add the robustness and positioning that let the tool stand on its own. The ladder above is ordered so that no step requires the authors to claim more than their evidence supports.
